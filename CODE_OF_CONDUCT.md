# Code of conduct

Veil is for people who want private calls. Treat everyone here with the same respect.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Do not share anyone's personal details without their consent.
- Keep discussion on Veil.

If someone breaks this, report it privately through the repository's Security tab
(Report a vulnerability) and say it is a conduct report. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, close issues, or block people who do not follow it.
